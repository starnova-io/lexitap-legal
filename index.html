Common questions

Tapping a word does nothing in Safari

Open Settings → Safari → Extensions and make sure LexiTap is turned on and has permission for the sites you are reading. iOS can switch extensions off after an update.

My cards are missing on another device

Cards sync through your LexiTap account, not iCloud. Sign in with the same account on both devices and pull to refresh the review screen.

I ran out of AI look-ups

Free accounts get a daily allowance of AI-powered definitions. The allowance resets every day; Pro removes it.

I bought Pro but it is not active

Open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases. If it still does not unlock, email us the Apple receipt and we will sort it out.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
